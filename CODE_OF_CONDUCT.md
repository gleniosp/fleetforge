# Code of conduct

## Participation

Treat contributors and users with respect in project issues, PRs, reviews, and
other project-managed spaces. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Keep feedback specific to the work, explain disagreements, and
accept corrections. Respect privacy and requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and
deliberate disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Technical disagreement does not justify personal attacks or retaliation against
someone raising a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and decisions

The repository maintainer, [gleniosp](https://github.com/gleniosp), owns conduct
decisions and moderation in project-managed spaces. For a concern that is safe
to discuss publicly, use a project issue with only the minimum context needed.
Do not post private evidence, identities of affected people without consent, or
details that could expose someone to further harm. For sensitive concerns,
request a private contact route without describing the incident publicly; wait
for an agreed route before sending details. Suspected software vulnerabilities
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

There is currently no dedicated confidential conduct contact, independent
reviewer, or formal appeal process. Establishing those routes, including a route
for complaints about the maintainer, remains a maintainer-owned policy decision.
GitHub's own reporting and blocking tools are available for abuse on GitHub;
they operate under GitHub's policies and do not provide an independent
FleetForge appeal process.

The maintainer assesses available information and may ask participants to change
behavior, moderate content, or restrict participation using repository controls.
Actions should reflect the conduct and its impact, with reasons explained to
affected participants where practical and without exposing private information.
Reporters can request reconsideration or provide additional information through
an agreed route. The project has no guaranteed response time or staffed
moderation service.
